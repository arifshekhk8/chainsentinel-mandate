## Submission checklist

- [ ] Eligible team and registration acknowledgment.
- [x] Working Topic F prototype with verifiable execution evidence and disclosed limits.
- [ ] Technical report covering architecture, agent design, security, deployment and evaluation.
- [ ] Presentation supporting a talk of no more than 10 minutes.
- [ ] Demonstration video of no more than 5 minutes.
- [ ] Source repository and README, security assessment, execution logs and transaction hashes.

The PDF marks the video optional and includes code/security/evidence in its checklist. The current website marks the video required and code/security/evidence optional. Prepare the full set to cover both. Templates can be downloaded when needed; they are not necessary for planning.

Finalists are announced on 31 October. Plan for Shenzhen on 20–23 November; invited finalist members receive organizer-arranged travel and accommodation. At least one team member must attend in person. Entry is free; the International Track prize pool is RMB 250,000 before tax.
